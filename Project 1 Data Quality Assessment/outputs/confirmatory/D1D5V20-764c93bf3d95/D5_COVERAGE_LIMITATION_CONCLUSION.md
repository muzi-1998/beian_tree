# D5 coverage-selection limitation

## Confirmatory conclusion

Full coverage contains 39,606 sensor-hours and Basic contains 46,046. Basic includes 29.1% OOD hours versus 0.0% in Full, and 74.8% L1-support hours versus 0.0% in Full.

The pooled standardized Full-minus-Basic differences are 0.187 for D1 and 0.158 for D2. 3 calendar months have no Full observations.

Full is therefore a complete-evidence subset selected by calendar time, regime/OOD state and D5 support. It must be reported as the primary complete-case estimand and must not be generalized to all sensor-hours. Basic remains a separately labelled extension analysis; Limited and Insufficient rows are excluded from formal composite comparisons.

Wasserstein distance and KS statistics are descriptive diagnostics only. This audit does not alter D5 scores or production eligibility.
